Education — First Aid & Health Lessons

WAY CARE is committed to empowering you with knowledge to prevent and manage health emergencies. Below are essential lessons and points for common symptoms and their first aid care:

  • Preventing and managing Severe Bleeding: Apply firm direct pressure with clean cloth, elevate the injured area above the heart to reduce blood flow, and call for emergency support if bleeding is heavy.
  • Choking first aid: Encourage coughing if conscious, perform 5 sharp back blows and 5 abdominal thrusts if choking persists and victim cannot breathe.
  • Burn management: Immediately cool the burnt area with running water for at least 20 minutes, avoid applying ice or creams, and cover loosely with sterile, non-stick dressing.
  • Heart attack awareness: Recognize symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath, and dizziness and call emergency services immediately.
  • Daily health and hygiene tips: Maintain a balanced diet, exercise regularly, avoid excessive stress, and prioritize sleep to enhance immunity.
  • COVID-19 hygiene: Wash hands frequently, wear masks where appropriate, maintain safe distancing, and get vaccinated.
